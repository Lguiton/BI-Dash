# Appendix C: Habits that carry across every field {-}

1. **Define before you compute.** Write down the question, the unit and the definition before touching a tool.
2. **Check cheap causes first.** Typos, caches, cables, filters and time zones explain more problems than exotic faults.
3. **Change one thing at a time** and write down what you changed.
4. **Keep a baseline.** Know what "normal" or "simple" gives you before you claim an improvement.
5. **Make work repeatable.** Scripts, version control and written steps beat memory.
6. **Write for the next person,** including you in six months.
7. **Back up and test the restore.** An untested backup is a hope.
8. **Least privilege and secrets out of code.** Give only the access needed; never commit keys.
9. **State uncertainty.** Say how sure you are and what would change your mind.
10. **Own your mistakes early.** A reported mistake is a lesson; a hidden one is an incident.
