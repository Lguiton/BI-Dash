# Company engagement: doing every discipline for one company

Hypothetically you are hired to take a company from "an idea and some data" to "it runs, it is governed, it is reported on".
That is eight disciplines, each its own field. **Company** (top of the dashboard) is the project plan for that job.

## The four phases
| Phase | Question | Typical owners |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | What is needed, is it feasible, what could go wrong, how is success measured? | Systems Analyst, Project & Product, Data Analyst, Governance |
| Design | What is the data model and the rules, in what order do we build, what is the API? | Systems Analyst, Governance, Project & Product, Full Stack |
| Build | Deliver in slices: pipeline, app, analysis, models, assistants. | Full Stack, Data Engineering, Scientist, ML, AI |
| Operate | Is it safe, healthy, on budget and proven against the requirements? | DBA, Governance, Project & Product, Systems Analyst, Analyst |

## How progress is decided
* **Detected**: proven by this workspace's data, for example 8+ requirements exist, three risks are registered, every table has
  an owner, or a backup has been taken. Nothing to tick.
* **Ticked by you**: work the app can't measure (an ER diagram you drew, a report you sent). Tick it when it is really done.
* Practice and Real keep **separate** plans, so a rehearsal never marks a real engagement as done.

## Using it
Fill in the brief (company, goal, notes), then follow **Next up**. Each deliverable links to the dashboard where it is done.
Every discipline card shows deliverables done and learning-path steps done, so you can see both the job and your own skill growth.
